  <h1>Time &amp; Time Transfer</h1>

  <p class="research-intro">
    Currently, microwave time transfer systems between ground and space reach a
    level of precision on the order of the nanosecond (about 1 to 30 ns achieved
    with GPS). An integration over ~30 days would be necessary to reach the
    10<sup>−15</sup> level. That cannot support the performance requirements and
    goals of future optical atomic clock in space missions (such as ESA-SOC),
    which aim for the 10<sup>−17</sup> level or better.
  </p>

  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/time-transfer-diagram.jpg' | relative_url }}"
       alt="Time and Time Transfer diagram"
       class="research-content-image">

  <p>
    Precise laser time- and frequency-transfer from the ground to an orbiting
    satellite would make it possible to improve upon the current state of the art
    in timing by roughly a factor of 1000, to the 1 ps level.
  </p>

  <p>
    This would also enhance the performance of existing systems and provide unique
    capabilities in navigation, precise timing, earth sciences, and geodesy, and
    the same approach could provide a platform for testing fundamental physics in
    space.
  </p>
