  <h1>Precision Farming/Agriculture</h1>

  <p class="research-intro">
    Stanford first demonstrated precision agriculture in the mid 1990's. The
    practice of precision agriculture was enabled by the advent of GPS and GNSS
    ability to locate precise position in a field for the creation of maps of the
    spatial variability of as many variables as can be measured.
  </p>

  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/gps-tractor.jpg' | relative_url }}"
       alt="GPS Tractor/Precision Farming photo"
       class="research-content-image">

  <p>
    The practice of precision agriculture allows the farmer to proactively manage
    crop performance in several ways:
  </p>
  <ul class="research-link-list">
    <li>Crop yield</li>
    <li>Terrain features/topography</li>
    <li>Organic matter content</li>
    <li>Moisture levels</li>
    <li>Nitrogen levels</li>
    <li>pH, EC, Mg, K, etc.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    Stanford originally received funding in this area from John Deere.
  </p>
